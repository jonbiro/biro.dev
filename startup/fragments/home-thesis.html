OUR PRODUCT THESIS

Knowing what to do
is only part of the work.

The next step has to feel possible.

Daily life involves more than keeping a list. It means deciding where to begin, remembering context, adjusting when plans change, and carrying responsibilities that other people may never see.

We’re developing tools that help make that work more manageable: clearer starting points, fewer decisions at once, and a way to pick things up again.
