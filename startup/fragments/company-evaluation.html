WHAT USEFUL SHOULD MEAN

Less work to use.
More support to act.

These are the questions we intend to use when evaluating product ideas and prototypes. They are development criteria, not measured results.

Does it make the next action clearer?

Someone should be able to understand the suggestion without first learning an elaborate organizing system.

Does it reduce work instead of adding it?

Capturing, correcting, and maintaining information should be considered alongside the benefit of a feature.

Can the person recover and change course?

Interruptions, uncertainty, and mistakes are part of everyday use. Pausing or choosing a different path should feel ordinary.

Does the person remain in control?

Suggestions, communication, personal information, and optional connections need understandable boundaries and choices.
