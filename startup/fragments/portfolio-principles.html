A CONNECTED VISION

Useful on its own.
Connected by choice.

The long-term goal is shared support without requiring the entire suite.

We envision optional sharing of accessibility preferences, authorized reminders, and relevant context across Biro.dev products. Each connection should be understandable, permission-based, and controlled by the person using it.

Affordability, privacy, accessibility by design, and offline functionality where practical are intended foundations of the product family. Core accessibility should be part of the experience, rather than a premium upgrade.

This is a future design direction. Cross-product connections and offline capabilities have not been released; pricing and technical details remain in development.
