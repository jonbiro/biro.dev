UNDERSTANDING THE PORTFOLIO

A shared mission.
Clear expectations.

Are all of these apps available?

No. AddvancedFocus is the flagship in development, with a scripted interactive concept on this website. The other products are future directions in early concept development. Their pages explain the intended experience; they are not working applications.

Would I need to use the whole family of apps?

No. The intended approach is for each app to be useful on its own. Any future sharing of preferences, reminders, or context should be optional, permission-based, and understandable.

How are you deciding what to build first?

AddvancedFocus remains the flagship. Future products would be evaluated around the everyday need, technical feasibility, accessibility, privacy, and the input required from the people who would use them. This portfolio is a direction, not a commitment to simultaneous launches.

Can I help shape a product?

Yes. Each product page includes a direct email link for sharing a perspective or question with Jonathan. You can describe the situation, the support that would help, and anything an app should avoid adding to your workload. There is no requirement to share a diagnosis or sensitive personal information.
