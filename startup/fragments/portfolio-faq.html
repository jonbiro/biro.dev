How are you deciding what to build first?

AddvancedFocus remains the flagship. OpenCall has a separate native iOS prototype, while the rest of the proposed products remain at different exploratory stages. We evaluate directions around everyday needs, feasibility, accessibility, privacy, and feedback. This portfolio is not a commitment to simultaneous launches.

Can I help shape a product?

Yes. Each product page includes a direct email link for sharing a perspective or question with Jonathan. You can describe the situation, the support that would help, and anything an app should avoid adding to your workload. There is no requirement to share a diagnosis or sensitive personal information.
