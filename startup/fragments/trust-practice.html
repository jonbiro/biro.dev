RESPONSIBLE ASSISTANCE

Trust comes from
understandable choices.

Assistive tools may be used in personal moments. These are the product decisions we intend to make explicit as the applications develop.

01

Know what is being shared

Explain the information needed for a feature, whether processing happens locally or through a service, and what is retained. Ask for permissions in the context where they matter.

02

Review the important details

Keep suggestions distinguishable from source information. Make room to correct a summary, verify a deadline, or reject a proposed action before relying on it.

03

Choose the level of support

Let people control what is communicated, who can access their information, and which connections they use. Caregiver involvement should have clear boundaries.

PRIVACY & ACCESSIBILITY QUESTIONS

What we can say today.

What happens to information entered into the website demo?

The AddvancedFocus prototype keeps selections and return notes in the current page’s memory. They are not sent to an AI service or saved to browser storage. Leaving the product page or refreshing clears them.

What happens when I email Biro.dev?

An email link opens your email app. The message is sent only when you choose to send it, and it passes through your provider and Biro.dev’s inbox. Contacting us does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Will the applications use local or cloud AI?

The technical design is not finalized. We intend to explain processing, retention, user controls, and the limits of offline functionality for each product before public release. No particular AI provider or processing arrangement is announced here.

Has this website been certified accessible?

No certification or completed conformance audit is claimed. WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the design target. The draft includes structural, keyboard, contrast, and reduced-motion provisions, but a complete browser and assistive-technology review is still required.

How can I report an accessibility barrier?

Email founder@biro.dev with the page and what made it difficult to use. If helpful and comfortable, include the device, browser, or assistive technology involved. A short description is enough to start the conversation.
