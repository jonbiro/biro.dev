MORE THAN A TASK LIST

The work you can see.
The load you can’t.

AddvancedFocus is intended to support both task initiation and the mental work of keeping everyday responsibilities moving.

PERSONAL FOLLOW-THROUGH

Make a difficult task approachable.

A large task can contain an unclear first step, setup work, and several decisions. The goal is to surface one concrete action that fits the time and energy available.

  • Separate the first step from the full project.
  • Adjust the effort without rebuilding the plan.
  • Leave a useful note when attention shifts.
HOUSEHOLD MENTAL LOAD

Give open loops a place to live.

Family logistics include noticing, planning, following up, and remembering what someone else is waiting for. We’re exploring ways to make that work visible and easier to revisit.

  • Clarify what needs attention and what can wait.
  • Distinguish your next action from a pending response.
  • Keep the context needed for a follow-up nearby.

A MANAGEABLE VIEW OF THE DAY

One useful action.
A place for everything else.

Four horizons organize the intended experience around how soon something needs your attention.

Now
One action to begin. Keep the immediate choice clear and the first step small.
Today
A short, realistic set of intentions. Leave room for the day to change.
Upcoming
Commitments and follow-ups that need attention soon, with enough context to prepare.
Later
Ideas and tasks that can wait. Keep them out of the immediate decision.

These horizons are demonstrated with scripted examples in the website concept. Persistent planning and AI-assisted prioritization are future product capabilities.
