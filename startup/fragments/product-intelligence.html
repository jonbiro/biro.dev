THE PRODUCT APPROACH

From an open loop
to an approachable action.

AddvancedFocus is being designed around a short, repeatable cycle: capture what is on your mind, choose a manageable action, and leave a clear path back.

An everyday intention

“I need to get ready for tomorrow, but I don’t know where to start.”

Useful context might include available time, energy, and what actually needs to happen.

An illustrative suggestion

Put the things you need
by the door.

Start with your keys and bag. Give it two minutes, then choose whether to continue.

Small enough to startEasy to adjust

A written example of the intended experience. No AI is running here.

01

Capture without perfect wording

The intended AI experience would help turn a loosely expressed need into a clear action. You should not need to build an elaborate system before it becomes useful.

02

Keep the choice with you

Review the suggestion, make it smaller, or choose a different priority. Your own understanding of the day should guide the plan.

03

Make returning part of the plan

Preserve the context that makes restarting easier. In the concept, a short return note gives the next attempt somewhere to begin.
