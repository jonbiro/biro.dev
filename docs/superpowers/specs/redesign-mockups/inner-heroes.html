How should inner pages fill their hero?

Today About, Products, Mission, Contact and Accessibility leave the right half of the hero empty on desktop. All three options keep the large editorial headline on the left. Shown here: the About page.

A

Constellation echo

Each page shows a small piece of the constellation tied to its subject. About: the founder's monogram at the center with the family around it. Products: the full family. Contact: one icon with an open orbit. It's consistent and recognizable, but icon-heavy.
