How should Editorial (A) and Constellation (C) combine?

Both use A's large type, thin rules and calm spacing. They differ in where the constellation lives. Each mockup shows the Home hero plus the start of the next band.

1

Constellation in the hero, status as a ruled strip

The headline sits left, the nine icons orbit on the right. A's "Where things stand" facts become a thin three-column strip under the hero, which is the first thing a reviewer scans after the headline.
