What's specified here

  • The constellation is decorative. The icons have empty alt text, and the whole figure is hidden from screen readers. The facts it implies (one flagship in development, eight concepts) are already in the text and the status strip, so nothing is lost.
  • The status strip replaces Home's current three-card status block. The full three-column block with the development stages stays on Products, and "Where things stand →" links to it.
  • The hero's old concept card and the ribbon artwork are retired on Home. The working demo gets the button, and the constellation becomes the visual.
  • On phones, the headline comes first, then the constellation at 280px, then the strip as a stacked list. The phone headline no longer breaks into short forced lines.
